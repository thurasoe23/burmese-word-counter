About counting Burmese

Why do normal word counters get Burmese wrong?

Burmese doesn't put spaces between words. Spaces mark phrases, so counting by spaces gives a number that is far too low.

Should I use words or syllables?

Word boundaries in Burmese are debatable, so different tools give slightly different word counts. Syllable counts are stable, which is why many translation jobs are priced per syllable.

What is Zawgyi?

Zawgyi is an older font encoding. It looks like Burmese but stores letters in a different order, so tools can't read it correctly. This page detects Zawgyi and can convert it to standard Unicode.

How does it work? Is my text private?

Words come from your browser's built-in Burmese dictionary (ICU). Syllables are split with rules based on Ye Kyaw Thu's sylbreak. Everything runs in your browser, and your text is never uploaded.

Is it free?

Yes. It's free, needs no sign-up, and works on phones and computers.
